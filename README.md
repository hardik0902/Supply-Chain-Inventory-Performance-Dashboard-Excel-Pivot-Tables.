# 🛍️ Vrinda Store — Annual Sales Dashboard 2022

> **Transforming raw retail data into actionable business insights using Microsoft Excel**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **annual sales analysis** for Vrinda Store — a multi-channel retail business selling clothing across India. The goal was to clean, analyze, and visualize sales data to help the store owner understand customer behavior, identify top-performing regions, and optimize sales strategy for 2023.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- Which month had the highest sales and orders?
- Who purchased more — Men or Women?
- What were the different order statuses in 2022?
- Which are the Top 5 states contributing to sales?
- Which age group places the most orders?
- Which sales channel contributes the most revenue?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

| Insight | Finding |
|---|---|
| 👩 Top Buyers | Women account for **64%** of total purchases |
| 📍 Top State | Maharashtra leads with **₹2.99M** in sales |
| 🛒 Top Channel | Amazon dominates with **35%** of orders |
| 🏆 Best Month | March recorded the highest sales & orders |
| ✅ Delivery Rate | **92%** of all orders were successfully delivered |
| 👩‍🦱 Top Age Group | Adult women (34.59%) place the most orders |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Sales by Top 5 States** — Bar chart comparing Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP, Telangana & Tamil Nadu
- **Men vs Women Sales** — Pie chart showing gender-wise purchase split
- **Orders by Channel** — Pie chart covering Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Meesho, Ajio & others
- **Age vs Gender Analysis** — Grouped bar chart across Adult, Senior & Teenager segments
- **Monthly Orders vs Sales Trend** — Combo chart tracking volume and revenue across all 12 months
- **Order Status Breakdown** — Delivered, Returned, Refunded & Cancelled orders

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data cleaning, formatting & analysis
- **Pivot Tables** — Aggregating data by month, channel, state, gender & age
- **Pivot Charts** — Bar, pie & combo chart visualizations
- **Slicers** — Interactive filters for Month, Channel & Category
- **Data Cleaning** — Handled inconsistent gender entries (e.g., "M/Men", "W/Women"), removed duplicates & nulls
